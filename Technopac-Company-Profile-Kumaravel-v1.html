How we help you select a machine

To recommend a suitable machine and prepare a quotation, please share:

  • Product name and a brief description
  • Packing format: pouch, bottle, jar, bag, tray, or carton
  • Pack weight or volume, and pouch or bag size, if you have it
  • Output you need, per minute, hour, or day
  • How the product behaves: powder, granule, liquid, paste, oily, sticky, fragile, or dusty
  • Preferred machine material, if you have one in mind
  • Photos or a short video of the product and the current pack

If a size or a detail is missing, send what you know. We will ask for the rest.
